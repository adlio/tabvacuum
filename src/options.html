Stale Tabs

Tabs are closed, not archived. Unsaved changes may be lost.

Automatic cleanup checks once an hour, in normal windows only. Turning it on closes nothing right away: the first check is at the next hour. It always keeps each window's active tab, pinned tabs, tabs playing audio, the last tab in a window, and tabs whose last viewed time is unknown.

After a browser restart, cleanup waits for the next hourly check and does not catch up. After your device sleeps, at most one check runs when it wakes; missed hours are not replayed. To recover a closed tab, use your browser's Reopen Closed Tab.
