Up and Down arrows move through results. Enter switches to the highlighted tab, or opens the highlighted history page. Tab opens the tab menu, where you can close tabs. Shift Tab reaches the Search in button, which chooses whether to also search history and page contents. Escape closes search.

Tab menu. Up and Down arrows, or J and K, move. Enter switches to the highlighted tab or opens the highlighted history page. X or Delete closes the highlighted tab; history results cannot be closed. M selects multiple tabs. Escape returns to search.

Select multiple mode. Up and Down arrows, or J and K, move. Space or Enter toggles selection of the highlighted tab. A, or Control A, selects all listed tabs. X or Delete closes the selected tabs. M or Done returns to the tab menu. Escape clears the selection and returns to the tab menu.
