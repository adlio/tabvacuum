Up and Down arrows move through results. Enter switches to the highlighted tab. Tab opens the tab menu, where you can close tabs. Escape closes search.

Tab menu. Up and Down arrows, or J and K, move. Enter switches to the highlighted tab. X or Delete closes the highlighted tab. M selects multiple tabs. Escape returns to search.

Select multiple mode. Up and Down arrows, or J and K, move. Space or Enter toggles selection of the highlighted tab. A, or Control A, selects all listed tabs. X or Delete closes the selected tabs. M or Done returns to the tab menu. Escape clears the selection and returns to the tab menu.
