    Up and Down arrows move through results. Enter switches to the highlighted tab, or opens the highlighted bookmark or history page. Tab opens the tab menu, where you can close tabs. Shift Tab reaches the search sources: Tabs, Bookmarks, History and Contents. Left and Right arrows move between them, and Space or Enter turns one on or off. Type @ and a source name, such as @b, then a space, to search only that source; @all searches every source. Escape closes search.

    Tab menu. Up and Down arrows, or J and K, move. Enter switches to the highlighted tab or opens the highlighted bookmark or history page. X or Delete closes the highlighted tab; bookmark and history results cannot be closed. M selects multiple tabs. Escape returns to search.

    Select multiple mode. Up and Down arrows, or J and K, move. Space or Enter toggles selection of the highlighted tab. A, or Control A, selects all listed tabs. X or Delete closes the selected tabs. M or Done returns to the tab menu. Escape clears the selection and returns to the tab menu.
